SEARCH STRATEGY - RSL LLM optimisation for edge / resource-constrained devices
==============================================================================

Databases      : Scopus (Elsevier) and Web of Science Core Collection (Clarivate)
Date limits    : none applied at search time (the 2024-2026 window results from
                 the screening stages, see docs/protocol.pdf)
Document type  : no filter at search time; EC-4 applied afterwards in
                 scripts/prisma_flow.py
Records        : Scopus = 586, Web of Science = 138, total = 724
Search fields  : [TO CONFIRM: e.g. Scopus TITLE-ABS-KEY / WoS Topic (TS)]
Search date    : [TO CONFIRM: YYYY-MM-DD]

Boolean query (identical in both databases, built from the PICOC blocks)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

("Large language model" OR "LLM*" OR "Foundation model*")
AND
("quantization" OR "model compression" OR "low-bit" OR "knowledge distillation" OR "pruning")
AND
("edge computing" OR "mobile" OR "resource-constrained" OR "edge device*" OR "FPGA" OR "smartphone")

Block mapping (PICOC)
---------------------
Block 1  Population    : large language models / foundation models
Block 2  Intervention  : algorithmic optimisation and compression techniques
Block 3  Context       : edge, mobile and resource-constrained hardware
Comparison and Outcomes were deliberately left out of the string to maximise
recall and capture every reported physical metric.

Known limitation
----------------
Parameter-efficient fine-tuning terms (LoRA, PEFT, low-rank) were not part of
the string. Low-rank studies were retrieved only when indexed under
compression or deployment terms (see Threats to Validity in the manuscript).

Raw exports

data/scopus_raw.csv  Scopus CSV export (abstracts, references, funding text
                     and e-mail addresses removed for copyright/privacy)
data/wos_raw.csv     Web of Science export in tabular (field-tag) form, same
                     redactions
